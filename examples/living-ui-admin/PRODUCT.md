# Product

## Outcome

Help a fictitious operations coordinator understand queue health, notice real changes and resolve exceptions without motion competing with the task.

## Experience

- visual system: shadcn/ui baseline unless product requirements override it;
- Motion Profile: `ambient` contextual by default;
- dense data/reading views: attenuate to `subtle`;
- reduced motion: mandatory for non-essential movement.

Use motion to communicate interaction, data changes, state, attention and navigation. Ambient effects are appropriate for login, waiting, empty states, headers and spacious surfaces when they do not compete with the task.

## First slice

- monitor queue capacity and operational summary;
- recheck data without replaying motion when values are unchanged;
- process a fictitious queue with observable processing and success states;
- review a pending exception in a focused side panel;
- retain the complete workflow with reduced motion enabled.

## Observable success

- the six semantic motion categories are represented with a clear function;
- attention stops after focus or opening the pending review;
- the capacity indicator changes only after a new value arrives;
- state labels and values remain understandable without animation;
- desktop and mobile remain accessible without horizontal overflow.

## Non-goals

- optional infrastructure without a product requirement;
- real user data or production credentials in examples;
- multiple design systems;
- continuous decorative animation in dense administrative screens.
